# Name candidates — {BRAND} = **Nullpoint**

> The product's promise is that no strategy is promoted until it survives a falsification gauntlet.
> The name should sound like a measurement instrument, not a trading app.

## The shortlist

| # | Name | Why it fits | Risk |
|---|------|-------------|------|
| **1** | **Nullpoint** | Two ideas in one word: **null hypothesis** (the thing every strategy must beat) and **null pointer** (the software/agents world). Short, two syllables, dark-instrument feel, `.com` plausible, no generic AI suffix. | "Null" can read as negative/nothing; needs the tagline to carry it. |
| 2 | Skepsis | Greek for inquiry; anchors the red-team/skeptic agent; calm academic tone. | Harder to say aloud; several philosophy projects use it. |
| 3 | Vernier | A precision instrument for fine measurement; short, technical, unclaimed in finance. | Vernier Science Education holds a broad mark in instruments. |
| 4 | Cairn | A human-made trail marker: verified evidence along a path. Calm, non-hype. | Crowded in outdoor brands; weak quant flavor. |
| 5 | Elenchus | The Socratic method of refutation — exactly what the skeptic agent does. | Spelling and pronunciation fail the "say it once" test. |

**Rejected:** Verity/Veritas, Axiom, Quorum, Tessera, Arbiter, Sharpe, Alpha*, Quant*, *AI (generic or
already held by large companies). Nothing named "EMPJID" appears anywhere in this repo.

## Winner: Nullpoint

- **Tagline:** *Nothing ships until it beats the null.*
- **One-sentence positioning:** Nullpoint is a multi-agent research platform where AI teams
  write, backtest and **falsify** systematic strategies, and only survivors of anti-overfitting
  validation reach the leaderboard.
- **Why it wins:** it names the core differentiator instead of the category. A competitor can copy
  "AI quant agents"; the brand promise is beating a null, which is testable — and we test it in CI.
- **Pronunciation:** NULL-point. Never "null point" in logo lockups; wordmark is one word.
- **Handle plan:** `@nullpoint` / `nullpoint.ai` / `nullpoint.com` fallback `nullpoint.io`,
  `getnullpoint.com`. Product subpaths: `nullpoint.dev` for docs.

## Checks you must run before spending on brand

1. **Trademark** — USPTO TESS + EUIPO + UKIPO for "Nullpoint" in classes 9 (software), 36 (finance),
   42 (SaaS/research). Search "Null Point" and phonetic equivalents ("Nulpoint"). Budget for a
   clearance opinion if any live mark sits in class 36/42.
2. **Domain** — `nullpoint.com`, `.ai`, `.io`, `.dev`. Check historical use (Wayback Machine) for
   spam/parked history before buying; avoid a domain with a manual penalty.
3. **Company registries** — Delaware/UK Companies House for "Nullpoint Inc/Ltd"; a registered
   company is not a trademark but raises conflict risk.
4. **App stores / GitHub orgs / npm / PyPI** — reserve `nullpoint` on GitHub, npm, PyPI and the
   social handles even if the `.com` is parked.
5. **Linguistic** — confirm the name is not unfortunate in your top 3 markets; "null" reads as
   "void" in German/Dutch, so keep the tagline visible in those markets.
6. **Legal copy** — file the mark *after* the clearance opinion; use ™ immediately, ® only after
   registration. Keep evidence of first commercial use.
